## Review workflow

1. Revise the outline with the user.
1. Generate a deck following explicit authorization.
1. Review and revise the deck.
1. Mark final only after user approval. Preserve reviewed versions and evidence
   boundaries.

Research claims must cite original evidence. Preserve negative results and distinguish
experiment tooling from research mechanisms. Future paper drafts require their own
outline and review process.
