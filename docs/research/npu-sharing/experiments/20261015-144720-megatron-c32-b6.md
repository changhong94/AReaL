reason `no_queued_work`.

## Interpretation and limits

The existing planner only selects native queued requests. These events mean that no
queued candidate existed at those snapshots, not that the dedicated engine had no
running work. This motivates R001, but does not prove that moving running requests would
help: replay, transfer and training contention can exceed the benefit. After 90.300 s,
both cards had completed inference; remaining time is not unused inference opportunity
for this finite workload.

Token counts are reconstructed from single-token decode-window completions; summary
audit reports no segment count differences. They are not exact emission timestamps.
Device-window spans are not exclusive hardware busy time.
