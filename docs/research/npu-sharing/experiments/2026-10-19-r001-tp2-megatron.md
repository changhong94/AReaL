# R001 TP=2 Megatron fixed/shared comparison — 2026-10-19

## Protocol

Formal `run_bench.py`, independent engine startup for each arm. No development vLLM
reuse. Four NPUs (0–3), Megatron, math RL, 48 questions, six answers per question, 16
concurrent questions, three batches, offpolicy=2, seed=1. Fixed uses two training NPUs
and one dedicated TP=2 inference instance. Shared adds a second TP=2 inference instance
on the training NPUs and enables the accepted moderated R001 KV policy. The same source
snapshot, model, data, image and observation switches apply to both. The comparison
measures the complete shared system, not isolated R001 benefit.

```bash
python3 /root/src/changhong94/AReaL/benchmark/npu_share/run_bench.py \
  --engine megatron --rl math --npu-count 4 --inference-tp 2 \
  --batches 3 --concurrency 16 --max-head-offpolicyness 2 --running-rebalance kv
```

Workspace: `/root/areal-benchmarks/20261019-162055-0700_math_megatron_c16_b3_67e4f4`.
[Extracted evidence and settings](2026-10-19-r001-tp2-megatron.json). Both containers
exited 0; all experiment NPU processes were released. The workspace contains four chart
families and bilingual summaries. The fixed token chart's reconstructed count is one
token below the answer log; totals below use answer logs.

Shared training took 3.763711 s less (3.69%), with 1.52% fewer output tokens. Including
initialization, shared took 16.491631 s more. This is one short paired run; it does not
establish repeatability, equal output work, or the isolated contribution of migration.
Initialization dominates this small workload and must not be hidden.

## Migration evidence

Two exploratory transactions moved two and one requests, respectively. All three
reported `import_applied`; these are confirmed imports rather than attempts. Whole
transaction times including planning were 1.205932 s and 0.512462 s. Total recorded
training-transition waiting for migration was 0.578333 s. Transaction wall time is not
pure HCCL transfer time and need not be entirely on the training critical path. No
policy parameters were changed during this comparison.
